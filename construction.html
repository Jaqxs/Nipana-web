Construction Supplies

The yard.
The real stuff.

People call when they need cement, steel, pipes, tools, or one fitting to finish a job. We keep the yard stocked in Mwanza and send things where the trucks can actually go.

This is not a showroom. It is a yard with shelves, bays, and trucks. The point is simple: keep the common items in stock, tell people the truth about what is there, and move it out without a song and dance.

What we keep

The things people
ask for most.

We do not try to sell everything. We keep the bits that move and leave the fancy talk out of it.

Cement and steel

Bagged cement, rebar, blocks, timber, aggregate, and the other basics sites run through quickly.

Tools and hardware

Drills, saws, hammers, bolts, gloves, helmets. The things that disappear from a job site first.

Pipes and fittings

PVC, PPR, copper, GI, valves, taps, toilets, sinks. The usual plumbing list.

Heaters and hot water

Water heaters, boilers, ducting, and fans for homes, offices, and small projects.

A little electrical

Cable, conduit, breakers, and switchboards for when the order needs the missing last piece.

Delivery

Pickup at the yard, or delivery if the load and the road make sense.

The yard

A working yard,
