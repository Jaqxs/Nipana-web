Two sides

Buying.
Selling.

We Buy

From people who mine it

We buy doré bars and rough gold from licensed Tanzanian producers and dealers we know. Four checks every time: who you are, where it came from, what it weighs, what it tests.

  • We check the mining licence
  • We record where it came from
  • We weigh in front of you
  • You're paid the same day
We Sell

To people who refine it

We sell to refineries and verified buyers under written contract. Every shipment goes with the paperwork — where it came from, what it tested at, and how it left the country.

  • A file showing where it came from
  • An independent assay
  • Bank-to-bank money
  • Insured shipping
How we stay clean

Four checks.
Always.

  • / 01

    Who you are

    National ID, business papers, who really owns the company. We file it and keep it for seven years.

  • / 02

    Where it came from

    Which licence, which site, signed by you. No source, no sale.

  • / 03

    The lists

    We check sanctions lists before we settle. If you're on one, we don't deal.

  • / 04

    The reports

    Big transactions go to the Financial Intelligence Unit on time. We don't skip the filings.

A day at the desk

In at nine.
Paid by five.

/ 01

You arrive

Bring the bar, bring your ID and licence. We open the seal in front of you.

/ 02

We test

Two tests in our own lab. If you want a third opinion from outside, we'll send a split.

/ 03

We price

Live London spot, less the refining fee and our margin. The numbers are on the wall, not in our heads.

/ 04

You leave paid

Bank transfer to your account, a receipt in your hand, and the tax forms filed.
