Why work at
NIPANA Atlas?

We are built on the idea that good work should be rewarded and that people should be trained to do their best. Most of our crew comes from the Lake Region, and we take pride in promoting from within.

At NIPANA, we don't just offer jobs; we offer careers. We invest in our people through technical training, safety education, and leadership development. Whether you're working in our mines or in our corporate office, you're part of a team that's building something that lasts.

The Departments

Where you
could be.

Our operations span multiple disciplines. We value expertise and a willingness to learn across all our teams.

/ 01

Mining Operations

From site managers to skilled miners, this team is the core of our output in Geita and Tabora. Safety and discipline are the priorities here.

/ 02

Gold Trading

Our desk team handles the buying, selling, and assaying of precious metals. Accuracy and integrity are non-negotiable.

/ 03

Logistics & Supply

Managing the wholesale yard and deliveries across the Lake Region. This team keeps the country's builders supplied.

/ 04

Corporate & Finance

The backbone of our governance. Handling our books, compliance, and strategic planning from our Mwanza base.

/ 05

Safety & Environment

Ensuring we meet our high standards for safety and land rehabilitation. A team dedicated to leaving things better.

/ 06

Human Resources

Finding the right people and ensuring they have the training and support to grow within the company.
