What you are backing

A real business,
not a promise.

The parts are plain enough. Mining, gold trading, construction supply. One group, one office, one balance sheet.

The mine is working

Geita is already producing. Tabora is still being drilled. Mwanza handles the recovery and trade.

The desk is real

Gold moves through our own desk in Mwanza. We keep track of where it comes from and where it goes.

The yard pays its way

Cement, steel, pipes, tools, heaters. It is the part that keeps cash moving when the pits are quiet.

The books close

We keep the paperwork close and the numbers simple enough to check without digging around.

How it hangs together

Three lines.

The honest bit

What can go
wrong.

  • Gold price can dip

    When the market moves, margins move with it. That is just part of the business.

  • Machines break

    A plant is still a plant. Pumps fail, belts wear out, and repairs cost money.

  • Rain slows work

    Access and hauling are always easier in the dry months.

  • South is still a question

    Drilling will tell us what is there. Until then, we do not pretend otherwise.
