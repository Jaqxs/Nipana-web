Mining

Working the belt.
One site at a time.

We mine gold in Geita and Tabora. Two mining sites, one team, and a working method built around careful drilling, disciplined processing, and land that gets restored as we go.

Geita and Tabora are the ground we know best. The sites run on one operating system: the same logging, the same checks, and the same standard every day.

The ground

Old rock.
New discipline.

We work narrow but rich veins, sampling as we go and keeping the paper trail clean. Every core, assay, and pour is stored so the story of the ground stays traceable.

The point is not speed for its own sake. The point is repeatable output, lower surprises, and a mine that stays in business long enough to matter.

The sequence

Ground to
gold bar.

The work is a loop, not a set of disconnected tasks. Each step feeds the next.

/ 01

Drill and sample

We drill on a tight grid and log every result before the pit moves forward.

/ 02

Mine and haul

The ore is lifted, sorted, and hauled with grade control so waste stays out of the stream.

/ 03

Crush and recover

North and Central handle the crushing, washing, and recovery work that makes the mine pay.

/ 04

Pour and record

Every pour is weighed, assayed, filed, and matched against the same records we keep in-house.

Working carefully

We mine like
we live here too.

  • / 01

    Safety first

    Eighteen months at Geita without a lost-time injury. The standard is to keep that going.

  • / 02

    Water control

    Wash water cycles back through ponds. We test downstream anyway, because trust has to be earned.

  • / 03

    Close as we go

    Finished benches are shaped, covered, and planted as part of the work, not after the fact.

  • / 04

    Community support

    We put money into schools and the health centre in Misungwi because the villages around the pits come first.
